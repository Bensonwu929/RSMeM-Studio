# Bundled example data

The GeoTIFF files under `examples/` are small subsets of **Earth-Bench**, created
by the Earth-Agent authors and distributed through
`Sssunset/Earth-Bench` on Hugging Face under the **Apache License 2.0**.

- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sssunset/Earth-Bench
- Project: https://github.com/opendatalab/Earth-Agent
- Paper: *Earth-Agent: Unlocking the Full Landscape of Earth Observation with Agents*
- License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Included subsets:

- `tvdi_sichuan/`: Earth-Bench question 2 (two GeoTIFFs)
- `nbr_california/`: Earth-Bench question 160 (22 GeoTIFFs)
- `nightlights_bhutan/`: Earth-Bench question 146 (two GeoTIFFs)
- `q10_black_forest/`: Earth-Bench question 10
- `q41_cyprus_ati/`: Earth-Bench question 41
- `q102_mumbai_lights/`: Earth-Bench question 102
- `q108_rosario_ndvi/`: Earth-Bench question 108
- `q124_dongting_turbidity/`: Earth-Bench question 124
- `q145_congo_rainfall/`: Earth-Bench question 145
- `q156_urmia_turbidity/`: Earth-Bench question 156
- `q161_california_hotspots/`: Earth-Bench question 161
- `q180_thailand_frp/`: Earth-Bench question 180
- `q187_shanghai_volume/`: Earth-Bench question 187

The RSMeM Studio code remains MIT-licensed. The bundled Earth-Bench data retains
its original Apache-2.0 license and attribution.
